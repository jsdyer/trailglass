    BC riding conditions

    Before you ride

    Compare recent station precipitation, Environment Canada forecasts, road access and Trailforks reports. Rainfall does not determine whether a trail is open. Follow local trail-association notices and on-site signs.

    Environment Canada · Historical climate observations (Open Government Licence – Canada) · DriveBC (OGL-BC)

    Trailforks status & recent reports

    Official Trailforks widgets keep their attribution and link back to the source. Reports may be old, incomplete or community-submitted. Their timestamps matter; a blank widget is not an open-trail confirmation.

    Open this region on Trailforks

    Loading these embeds connects your browser to Trailforks and its service providers. If an embed is blocked, use the region link.

    Track inspector

    Load a Garmin or other GPX activity to see distance, recorded ascent, elevation and elapsed time.

    Estimate terrain elevation

    Get a separate modelled profile for this route. Clicking sends up to 100 sampled coordinates to Open-Meteo; the GPX file itself stays on your device.

    Open-Meteo elevation API · Copernicus DEM GLO-90 · CC BY 4.0

    The export contains your track coordinates. Share it only with the people you choose.

    Sources and map limitations

    Routes are municipal line segments. They show mapped features, and some Halifax lines are suggested routes. Langley’s layer includes trails with different uses. Check local signage, current conditions and access rules before riding.

    Municipal source details load when a layer is selected.
